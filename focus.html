Research

Adaptive Sequence Horizon

Being from a Computer Science background, much so a fresh graduate, I am naturally attracted to solving complex problems using computer algorithms. Outside my usual cloud and systems work, I've been spending time experimenting with local patterns in binary sequences.

My current project, Adaptive Sequence Horizon, explores whether a sequence that looks random globally can still contain temporary, locally predictable structure.

I'm building an adaptive algorithm that changes how much recent history it considers, analyzes the current local regime, selects a target bit and estimates the probability of that bit appearing within a future horizon. I'm testing the idea through strict walk-forward experiments so every prediction is made before the future observations are revealed.

It's experimental research and very much a work in progress. If sequence analysis, adaptive algorithms or finding structure in noisy data sounds interesting, check out the project.
